- The Crafting Upgrade now comes with the vanilla recipe book. Recipes you have the items for in storage show as craftable, and clicking one fills the crafting grid from storage first, then your inventory. Shift-click fills as many as possible.
- The book button under the crafting arrow shows or hides the recipe book, the same as on a crafting table.
- The crafting tab moved to the right side of the menu. The crafting grid still opens on the left, with the recipe book under it.
- The upgrade slots are now one column on a strip beside your inventory, and the "Upgrades" label is gone.
- The fullness bar and percentage moved under the menu. A new setting shows or hides it (it only changes it for you).
- The module count is shown in the settings panel.
- The help (?) button moved into the settings panel.
- The settings panel's crafting section is just called "Crafting" now.

Requires Fabric Loader 0.20.1+ and Fabric API. Install on both the client and the server.
